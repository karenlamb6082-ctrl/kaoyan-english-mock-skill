# Mock 01-06 Ecology Ledger

Read this reference before designing Mock 06 or when auditing theme diversity, conflict repetition, difficulty, item mix, or writing overlap in the current series.

## Overall Finding

Mock 01 established structure, Mock 02 improved policy boundaries, Mock 03 produced the strongest full-paper ecology, and Mock 04 produced the strongest individual items. The remaining weakness is cross-paper and within-paper sameness: different surface topics repeatedly resolve into measurement vs value, formal oversight vs real control, universal systems vs marginal groups, and technology benefits constrained by institutional governance.

Do not make the next paper harder by increasing rare vocabulary or abstract nouns. Increase evidence span, viewpoint boundaries, author attitude, and distractor precision.

## Three Principal Problems

1. Topic deduplication does not yet remove conflict and argument-template repetition.
2. Increasing difficulty has sometimes meant increasing abstraction instead of evidence distance.
3. Review proves that correct options work but does not yet prove that every wrong option is competitive and uniquely wrong.

Reusable lessons:

4. If a final-page table or Cloze row spills alone, reduce row padding before changing font size. Start Part C on a new page when its heading and directions would otherwise be stranded.
